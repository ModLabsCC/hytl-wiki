## Choose the Trork variant

“Trork” covers several creature records. Equipment with Trork in its name is not a creature spawn record. Search for the variant and inspect its Spawn locations section when you need a place to look.

The observed release records connect the Trork Chieftain to **The Fens**, the Trork Hunter to **Seedling Woods**, and the Trork Mauler to both regions. These are recorded location relationships, not a promise that a particular individual will be present when you visit.

## Inspect the region and the creature separately

Follow :zone[The Fens]{id="Zone1_Tier3"} or :zone[Seedling Woods]{id="Zone1_Tier2"} for the corresponding region record. The creature page carries its own recorded spawn and drop information. A region association alone does not establish a spawn rate, an encounter count or a drop probability.

## Use the recorded drops

If you are searching for a material rather than the creature itself, inspect the creature's drop section and the material's sources. Keep those two questions separate: where a creature is recorded and what its drop data contains.
