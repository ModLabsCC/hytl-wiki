## Power and the Goblin Gateway

Start with the generator. Its repair grants power to the lab; the Gateway, Console and recipe-unlock machines each require that power.

| Device | Recorded repair cost |
| --- | --- |
| :item[Goblin Power Generator]{id="GoblinLab_Generator"} | 10 × :item[Scraps]{id="Ingredient_Scraps"} |
| :item[The Goblin Gateway]{id="GoblinLab_Fragment_Portal"} | 4 × :item[Void Crystal Shards]{id="Ingredient_Crystal_Void"} |

The **Goblin Gateway repair** consumes four Void Crystal Shards. Keep this separate from a Console upgrade and from crafting a portable fragment: each has its own material list.

## Upgrade the Breach Console

The :item[Goblin Breach Console]{id="GoblinLab_Console"} has three successive recorded upgrades. Each requires lab power. Plan for the material cost of the specific next upgrade shown by your machine.

| Console upgrade | Cost for this step |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 × :item[Scraps]{id="Ingredient_Scraps"}, 2 × :item[Void Crystal Shards]{id="Ingredient_Crystal_Void"} |
| 2 | 8 × :item[Void Crystal Shards]{id="Ingredient_Crystal_Void"}, 2 × :item[Voidheart]{id="Ingredient_Voidheart"} |
| 3 | 5 × :item[Voidheart]{id="Ingredient_Voidheart"}, 4 × :item[Enkindled Sigil]{id="Ingredient_Enkindled_Resource"}, 2 × :item[Bundled Treasure]{id="Ingredient_Deep_Resource"} |

The Console extends breach visits. These repair records do not give a complete upgraded timer calculation, so this table does not promise a duration in minutes.

## Restore recipe-unlock machines

The :item[Goblin Tinkerer's Workbench]{id="GoblinLab_Tinkering_Bench"} uses separate lab unlocks for its recipes. Boiler, Compressor and Engine all require power, and each grants a different unlock. The Deepworks fragment specifically requires the **Compressor** unlock. The Engine repair also asks for a Gobliterator Core, so it belongs to a later material goal.

| Machine | Recorded repair cost |
| --- | --- |
| :item[Boiler]{id="GoblinLab_Tinkering_Bench_Recipeset_A"} | 15 × :item[Scraps]{id="Ingredient_Scraps"}, 3 × :item[Dubious Assembly]{id="Ingredient_Gadget_Assembly"}, 2 × :item[Spider Silk]{id="Ingredient_Spider_Silk"} |
| :item[Compressor]{id="GoblinLab_Tinkering_Bench_Recipeset_C"} | 75 × :item[Scraps]{id="Ingredient_Scraps"}, 3 × :item[Voidheart]{id="Ingredient_Voidheart"}, 2 × :item[Enkindled Sigil]{id="Ingredient_Enkindled_Resource"}, 1 × :item[Bundled Treasure]{id="Ingredient_Deep_Resource"} |
| :item[Engine]{id="GoblinLab_Tinkering_Bench_Recipeset_B"} | 150 × :item[Scraps]{id="Ingredient_Scraps"}, 30 × :item[Boom Powder]{id="Ingredient_Powder_Boom"}, 1 × :item[Voidtaken Grizzly Hide]{id="Ingredient_Voidtaken_Grizzly_Hide"}, 1 × :item[Gobliterator Core]{id="Ingredient_Gobliterator_Core"} |

### Crafting Dubious Assemblies

The Boiler repair needs three :item[Dubious Assembly]{id="Ingredient_Gadget_Assembly"}. The recipe below makes one assembly from 25 Scraps and two Void Crystal Shards, with lab power required. For all three assemblies, set aside **75 Scraps and six shards**, in addition to the Boiler's direct repair cost.

## Craft the Deepworks fragment

After restoring power and repairing the Compressor, the recorded recipe for :item[Fragment: Orbis - The Deepworks]{id="PortalKey_The_Deepworks"} requires **30 Scraps, five Void Crystal Shards and one Voidheart** at the Goblin Tinkerer's Workbench. The fragment's crafting ingredients are separate from the Compressor repair materials.

```hytl:recipe
{
  "item": "PortalKey_The_Deepworks"
}
```
The recipe requires both the lab-power and Compressor unlocks. If the recipe remains unavailable, check those repairs and your Chapter objectives before gathering another set of ingredients.

## Dungeon and boss spoilers

The Deepworks is a handcrafted dungeon with checkpoints, puzzles and environmental hazards. Its encounters include the **Feastmaster** and the multi-phase **Gobliterator**. These names and the dungeon outline are confirmed in the [official Chapter I release post](https://hytale.com/news/2026/10/2026-10-12-chapter-i-out-of-the-breach/).
