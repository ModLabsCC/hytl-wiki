The Emerald Wilds contains the named regions **Seedling Woods** and **The Fens**. Both are linked to copper- and iron-bearing stone in the release records. Their creature lists differ, making the region name more useful than searching only for “Zone 1”.

The Trork Hunter is associated with Seedling Woods, the Trork Chieftain with The Fens, and the Trork Mauler with both. For a Trork search, choose the creature you need before setting out; a matching piece of Trork armour is an item, not a spawn location.
